BODY COMPOSITION

Lean out without flattening performance.

Until race day

No crash cut. Eat enough carbohydrate around workouts and keep protein high. Performance comes first for two weeks.

Protein

Use roughly 1.8–2.2 g/kg/day as the practical range.

After race

Resume a modest 5–10% calorie deficit if waist loss is the priority. Track weekly-average weight + waist, not single-day scale noise.

PRODUCTIVITY

Simple enough to repeat.

1 must-win task

Decide it before leaving home. When work allows, make it the first protected focus block.

2 secondary tasks

Useful, but they do not steal the morning from the must-win.

One personal block

Admin, learning, finances, chores or a project. Pick one category for 30–45 minutes. Do not bounce between five things.
