WORKOUT EVOLUTION

Easy first. Then economy, tempo, VO₂.

Weeks 1–6 · Foundation

Easy running only. Add relaxed 15–20 sec strides. Mileage grows from 12 to 20 before a cutback.

Weeks 7–8 · Economy

Add short repetition/hill work with full recovery. Thursday becomes a fifth easy run if the lifting and injury gates are green.

Week 9 · Calibration

A 3K benchmark on a flat road or treadmill sets current VDOT-style training paces. Old PRs do not set current workouts.

Weeks 10–13 · Threshold / Tempo

3×5 min T → 2×8 min T → cutback → controlled 20-minute tempo.

Weeks 14–17 · 5K Development

Friday alternates 3–5 minute Interval work and Threshold work while Tuesday remains easy + strides.

Week 18 · Race / benchmark window

Late February is the full-build target. A December or January race can happen earlier only if your checkpoints support it.
