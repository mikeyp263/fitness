Lower-stomach truth

Train abs; lose fat globally

Ab work strengthens and thickens the abdominal wall, but it cannot reliably choose where fat leaves first. One small 2023 male-only trial suggested localized trunk-fat loss, while an earlier randomized trial found ab training improved endurance without reducing abdominal fat. That is not strong enough evidence to promise lower-belly spot reduction.

The dependable approach is the plan you now have: resistance training to preserve/build muscle, 305 prescribed aerobic minutes across the 14 days, daily movement, and a controlled calorie intake.
