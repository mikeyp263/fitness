Why this split

Weekly volume matters more for hypertrophy than the name of the split. Arms get ~12 direct biceps sets and ~12 direct triceps sets per week, spread across multiple sessions. Abs get ~15 loaded sets. Chest, back and legs keep enough volume to maintain or build rather than being sacrificed.

Frequency is mainly being used to distribute volume and keep set quality high; when volume is equated, frequency itself is not a magic hypertrophy variable.

Scientific sources

Fat-loss phase in resistance-trained athletes — target 0.5–1.0% bodyweight loss/week; 2.2–3.0 g/kg protein.

Protein during caloric restriction — higher protein supports lean-mass retention.

2026 resistance-training dose-response meta-regression — more weekly volume generally improves hypertrophy with diminishing returns.

Concurrent aerobic + resistance training meta-analysis — aerobic work does not inherently prevent hypertrophy/max strength when managed.

Abdominal exercise and abdominal fat — ab work does not spot-reduce stomach fat.
