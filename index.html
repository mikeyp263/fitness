How the plan evolves

Not random workouts. Each 12-week wave changes the quality you emphasize.
Weeks 1-3 - FoundationModerate 6-12 rep lifting. Landing and acceleration mechanics. Low plyometric volume.
Week 4 - DeloadAbout one-third less work. Everything stays crisp.
Weeks 5-7 - ForceMain strength shifts toward 3-6 reps. Jumps and acceleration become more demanding.
Week 8 - DeloadReduce fatigue before speed emphasis.
Weeks 9-11 - SpeedMain lifts use lower volume and fast 2-5 rep sets. Sprint, reactive jump and agility quality rise only when your back is ready.
Week 12 - Test + ResetLow volume. Assess safe athletic markers, recover, then begin the next wave.
Muscle stays in the plan year-round. We are just not organizing your entire life around bodybuilding. Arms, shoulders, chest and back still receive direct hypertrophy work. The plan requires no sled and no medicine ball.
