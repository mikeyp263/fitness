Week-to-week logic

Week 1 · Baseline

Establish honest working loads. Every next exposure targets at least +1 total clean rep.

Week 2 · Rep accumulation

Same anchor lifts. Beat Week 1 rep totals. If every set reaches the top of the range, increase load next exposure.

Sep 7–20 · Growth Push

Priority biceps/delts receive +1 quality set on selected movements while progression continues.

Sep 21–30 · Intensification

Extra sets come back out. Keep volume stable and push load/rep PRs rather than adding junk volume.

What “progress” means

REP PR: same load, higher total reps.

LOAD PR: all sets reached the top of the range, so the app instructs you to add weight.

HOLD: a poor-sleep/back day can maintain or reduce load. One session never overrides the multi-week trend.

Waist reduction system

Lower-belly fat comes down by reducing total body fat.

R14 cannot choose where your body loses first. It can control the deficit, activity, muscle retention and feedback.
