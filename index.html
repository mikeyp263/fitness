NOT A BODYBUILDING SPLIT

Train qualities, not mirror muscles.

Strength supports power. Muscle supports durability. Sprinting, jumping, acceleration, deceleration, lateral movement and conditioning are trained as skills. The goal is an athletic body because you train like an athlete.

12-week wave

1
Weeks 1-3 - Rebuild
Movement quality, landing mechanics, low plyometric dose, moderate strength. No max sprinting.
2
Week 4 - Absorb
Cut lifting volume about 35%. Keep speed crisp but low volume.
3
Weeks 5-7 - Force
Heavier 3-6 rep strength work, short accelerations, more horizontal and vertical power.
4
Week 8 - Absorb
Lower fatigue. Keep intent fast.
5
Weeks 9-11 - Speed
Lower lifting volume, fast 2-5 rep work, faster sprints, reactive jumps and change of direction.
6
Week 12 - Test + Reset
Low volume. Test safe markers such as broad jump, vertical jump and short acceleration if your back is ready.

Long-term rule

Repeat waves with harder variations only when you own the current level. As you age, the plan keeps power, sprint exposure, strength, balance and aerobic work instead of gradually becoming lifting-only.

10-15 MINUTES

Movement prep

Reset90/90 breathing · pelvic tilts · cat-camel
Mobilize90/90 hip switches · hip-flexor stretch · adductor rockbacks · ankle rocks
ActivateGlute bridge · lateral band walk · bird dog · Pallof press
PrimeMarching · skips or low pogo contacts only when pain-free and ready
Your back is the gatekeeper. Sprinting, hard cutting and aggressive jumping only progress when normal movement, easy running and low-level jumping are symptom-free. Sharp/radiating pain, numbness or weakness means stop and get assessed.
