RIR is shown beside every exercise. 2 RIR = stop when you could do about 2 more clean reps. 0–1 RIR = the set ends at technical failure or one clean rep before it. Technical failure means the next rep would require cheating, shortened range, or losing position.
Pain is not failure. Sharp pain or back/hip pain that changes your movement means stop that exercise.

Zone 2 instructions

Default: incline treadmill. Start near 3.0–3.4 mph and 6–10% incline, then adjust until breathing is elevated but you can still speak in short sentences. RPE about 3–4/10.

Bike or elliptical are equally fine. No intervals. If incline bothers your back/hip, lower it or change machines.

Progression

When every work set reaches the top of its rep range at the listed RIR with clean form, add the smallest useful load next time. If reps miss the range, keep the same load.

Back / hip foundation

After Thursday cardio: pelvic tilts 1×15, cat-camel 1×8, half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch 2×40 sec/side, glute bridge 2×12, bird dog 2×6/side.
