Chicken dinner

Use 8 oz cooked chicken breast instead of beef. Because chicken is leaner, start with 1¾ cups cooked rice or about 2¼ cups roasted potatoes, plus 1½ cups vegetables, 1¼ cups 0% Greek yogurt and 1 scoop whey. Use the ¼-cup carb adjustment if needed.

If you hate that much food at dinner

Keep the two-meal structure, but move ¼–½ cup of the dinner rice/potatoes into breakfast by adding another ¼–½ cup of oats. Total calories stay the same; breakfast just gets even bigger.

Performance override: if two lifting sessions in a row are clearly worse, take 1 scoop whey + a banana before the next lift and remove roughly the same calories from breakfast/dinner later. Training quality matters more than preserving the fast.
