Progressive overload system

Never repeat the same performance on purpose.

Each exercise stores your last load and reps. The next exposure tells you what to beat.

Progression rules

Double progression: use the listed rep range. First add reps with the same load. When every working set reaches the top of the range cleanly, increase the smallest available load next time.

Compounds: normally finish with 1–2 reps in reserve. Isolation: 0–2 RIR; only the final safe isolation set may reach technical failure.

Bad day rule: if sleep, back symptoms or performance are clearly poor, hold or reduce load. Progression is measured over weeks, not forced every session.

Recomp nutrition

Simple food. Repeatable for six weeks.

2,300starting calories
190–210 gprotein
3–4 Lwater
8+ hrsleep target

Calories are adjusted by your 7-day weight trend, waist, hunger, recovery and gym performance. We are not trying to crash the scale.

Simple daily food

Breakfast after training

Eggs + egg whites, oats + banana, protein shake.

Work

Greek yogurt + protein bar if needed. If fasting at work feels better and training remains strong, keep it simple and eat later.

Dinner

10 oz cooked chicken or 93/7 beef + measured rice/potatoes + frozen vegetables.

Emergency breakfast

Protein shake + banana. Missing a cooked breakfast never ruins the day.

Adjustment logic

Hold calories for at least 7 consistent days before reacting. If average weight and waist are flat for roughly 2 weeks, reduce about 100–150 calories. If weight falls rapidly and strength/recovery worsen, add about 100–150.
