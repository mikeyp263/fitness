R14 Transformation

Build bigger muscle. Lose fat. Protect the back. Repeat long enough to look completely different.
High intensity · recoverable volume

Physique Specialization

Biceps, delts, upper chest, lats and abs get priority. Intensity comes from progression and hard sets, not random extra volume.

Progression rules

Compounds: stop with 1–2 clean reps in reserve. Isolation: 1 RIR; final safe isolation set can reach technical failure.

When every set reaches the top of its rep range with clean form, increase the smallest available load next time. Log loads.

Rest 2–3 min on compounds and 75–120 sec on isolation work.

Recomp · simple food

Start at 2,300 kcal / 200 g protein

Enough deficit to pursue fat loss without turning the plan into starvation. Judge by 7-day averages and training performance.

2,300starting calories
190–210 gprotein
3–4 Lwater
8+ hrsleep target
Meal template

Breakfast: eggs/egg whites + oats + banana + protein shake.

Work option: Greek yogurt + protein bar when needed. Fasting at work is optional, not required.

Dinner: 8–10 oz chicken or 93/7 beef + measured rice/potatoes + a large serving of vegetables.

Still hungry: Greek yogurt, fruit, extra vegetables or a protein shake. Do not routinely go to bed ravenous.

Adjustment rule

Hold the target for 7 consistent days before judging it. If average weight/waist do not trend down for 2 consistent weeks, reduce about 100–150 kcal. If performance, recovery and energy clearly deteriorate while weight falls quickly, add about 100–150 kcal.
