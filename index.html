Your hybrid week

Five real lifting days. Two real athletic days. The goal is muscular and shredded, but fast, explosive, conditioned and mobile too.
Sunday - Push · GYMChest, shoulders, triceps + a little explosive pressing.
Monday - Pull · GYMBack, rear delts and biceps + easy cardio finish.
Tuesday - Speed + Agility · OUTSIDESprint mechanics, acceleration, lateral quickness, braking, change of direction and conditioning.
Wednesday - Legs + Power · GYMJumps first, then quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves and trunk.
Thursday - Arms + Delts · GYMHigh-priority biceps, triceps, side delts and rear delts.
Friday - Back + Chest · GYMSecond major torso session for size, strength and definition.
Saturday - Athletic Conditioning + Hip/Back · OUTSIDE / HOMEZone 2, relaxed strides when ready, low-level elastic work and your hip/back foundation.

How the plan evolves

The goal never changes: get lean and visibly muscular while also improving sprinting, quickness, jumping, conditioning, hip function and back resilience.
Weeks 1-3 - FoundationModerate 6-12 rep lifting. Landing and acceleration mechanics. Low plyometric volume.
Week 4 - DeloadAbout one-third less work. Everything stays crisp.
Weeks 5-7 - ForceMain strength shifts toward 3-6 reps. Jumps and acceleration become more demanding.
Week 8 - DeloadReduce fatigue before speed emphasis.
Weeks 9-11 - SpeedMain lifts use lower volume and faster 2-5 rep sets, but chest, arms, delts and back accessories stay in. Sprint, reactive jump and agility quality rise only when your back is ready.
Week 12 - Test + ResetLow volume. Assess safe athletic markers, recover, then begin the next wave.
Muscle stays a major priority year-round. Chest, back, shoulders, arms and legs get direct hypertrophy work every week. Sprinting, jumping, cardio and hip/back work are added so you also stay quick, explosive and capable. No sled or medicine ball required.
