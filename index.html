Recomp nutrition

Simple Enough to Repeat for Months

Muscle growth is the training priority. Fat loss is gradual. The scale does not need to crash.

Block 1 starting targets

2,450Calories/day starting target
200 gProtein/day target

Acceptable protein: 190–210 g. Water: 3–4 L/day. Keep sodium normal and consistent.

We adjust calories from the 7-day weight average, waist and gym performance—not from one weigh-in.

Breakfast · after training

3 whole eggs + 1 cup liquid egg whites

Oatmeal + banana

1 scoop protein powder. Water by default; milk only when it fits the day's calories.

Work food

High-protein Greek yogurt + protein bar. Add a piece of fruit if needed.

Dinner

10 oz cooked chicken or 93/7 ground beef + 1.5 cups cooked rice + frozen vegetables.

Season it. Measure calorie-dense sauces instead of guessing.

Emergency breakfast · no time

Protein shake + banana immediately. Take Greek yogurt/protein bar with you. Missing a cooked breakfast never becomes a reason to abandon the day.

Core groceries

Eggs · liquid egg whites · oatmeal · bananas · protein powder · high-protein Greek yogurt · protein bars · chicken and/or 93/7 beef · rice · frozen vegetables · simple seasonings/sauce.

Adjustment rule

Do not change calories during the first 7 consistent days. After that: if waist and average weight are not moving at all for 2 weeks, reduce by about 100–150 calories. If weight is dropping quickly and gym performance/recovery deteriorate, add about 100–150 calories.

Physique priorities

Biceps · Delts · Upper Chest · Lats · Abs

Five lifting days. Saturday recovery. Sunday completely off. Monday always starts Push.

How every set works

Compounds: 1–2 reps in reserve. Isolation: 0–2 RIR; final safe isolation set may reach technical failure.

Rest: 2–3 minutes compounds, 90–120 seconds isolation.

Progression: stay in the listed range. Once every working set reaches the top cleanly, increase the smallest available load.

Back: supported/upright work whenever possible. No loaded bending just to chase numbers.

Daily check-in

14-day checkpoint

Photos: front, side and back in the same lighting. Measure waist and flexed arm. Compare average body weight, gym performance and back symptoms.

First checkpoint: Aug 26. Then roll directly into Block 2—no restart.
