Simple food · repeat it

Transformation Menu

The next 10 days are intentionally boring. High protein, controlled calories, enough carbohydrate to train hard.

Daily target

2,400–2,500Calories
190–210 gProtein

Water: 3–4 L/day. Keep sodium normal and consistent. Do not dehydrate yourself.

Meal 1 · after gym

3 whole eggs + 1 cup egg whites

1 cup dry oatmeal cooked with water + 1 banana

1 scoop Body Fortress with water or milk that fits the calorie target.

Meal 2 · simple work food

1 high-protein Greek yogurt + 1 protein bar

Use the same brands all week so calories are predictable.

Meal 3 · dinner

10 oz cooked 93/7 ground beef or chicken

1.5 cups cooked rice

2 cups frozen vegetables

Season normally. Use measured sauce if wanted.

If hungry

Add fruit, zero-calorie drinks, or another serving of low-calorie vegetables first. If protein is short, add Greek yogurt or a half/full shake while staying inside calories.

Buy this

Eggs · liquid egg whites · oatmeal · bananas · Body Fortress · high-protein Greek yogurt · protein bars · 93/7 ground beef and/or chicken · rice · frozen vegetables · seasonings/sauce.

That is the core grocery list. No special recipes required.

10-day rules

No alcohol. No liquid calories other than planned milk/shakes. No cheat day. One off-plan meal does not become an off-plan day. Log portions honestly.

Physique priority

Arms · Delts · Upper Chest · Lats · Abs

Train hard enough to progress, not so hard that soreness destroys the next session.

Hypertrophy rules

Working sets: warm-ups do not count.

Effort: compounds 1–2 reps in reserve. Isolation work 0–2 reps in reserve; only the final safe isolation set may reach technical failure.

Rest: 2–3 min compounds, 90–120 sec isolations.

Tempo: controlled lowering, full comfortable range, no lower-back cheating.

Progress: if all sets reach the top of the rep range cleanly, add the smallest load next time.
