Nutrition

Exact target appears on Today

Protein never changes: 195 g every day. Calories and carbs rise only when training demand or the final peak calls for it.

What “burn” means

Active burn = the active-calorie number from your watch/fitness tracker. It is not your total daily calorie burn. Use the range as a guardrail, not a contest. If you finish the prescribed workout/cardio, do not add random extra cardio just because the watch is low.

Base meals around lean meat/fish, eggs/egg whites, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese or whey. Use rice, potatoes, oats, fruit, bread/wraps and rice cakes for carbs.

Example 2,300-calorie day

Breakfast: 2 eggs + 250 g egg whites + oatmeal + banana.

Lunch: chicken breast + measured jasmine rice + vegetables + measured sauce.

Meal 3: 0% Greek yogurt + whey + berries + rice cakes.

Dinner: lean beef/turkey/chicken/fish + measured potato/rice + vegetables.

Adjust carb portions to hit the day’s calorie target. Never reduce protein to make the calories fit.

Non-negotiables

  • 195 g protein daily.
  • 5 g creatine daily.
  • 3–4 L water daily.
  • 25–35 g fiber most days.
  • 8+ hours sleep target.
  • No alcohol through September 4.
  • No untracked cheat day.
  • Measure oils, sauces, nut butter and calorie-dense snacks.
  • Do not eat back watch calories.

Adjustment rule

Use your 7-day average weight plus waist and strength. If loss is roughly 0.5–1.0% of bodyweight per week and strength is stable, change nothing. If weight/waist are not moving after a consistent week, cut ~100–150 kcal. If loss is >1%/week and strength/recovery are clearly dropping, add ~100–150 kcal.
