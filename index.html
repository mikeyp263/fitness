Why this split is optimized for your goal

Arms receive about 10 direct biceps sets and 10 direct triceps sets/week, plus indirect work from pulling and pressing. Abs receive 12 loaded sets/week. Chest, back and legs keep enough hard work to preserve your overall muscularity.

The split name is not magic. Weekly hard-set volume, progression, exercise quality, and recovery matter more. The extra frequency simply spreads your priority volume so the sets stay high quality.
