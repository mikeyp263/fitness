Lower-belly strategy

We cannot choose exactly where fat comes off first.

So this plan attacks the things we can control: total fat loss, waist reduction, abdominal muscle thickness, activity, and muscle retention/growth.

Fat-loss feedback loop

1 · Weigh every morning

Bathroom first, before food/drink. Ignore single-day jumps. The 7-day average is what matters.

2 · Waist every Monday

At the navel, relaxed, same conditions. Lower-belly progress is judged primarily by waist + photos, not scale weight alone.

3 · Target trend

Aim for roughly 0.5–1.0 lb/week while strength is stable or improving. Faster is not automatically better when muscle growth matters.

4 · Calorie adjustment

After 14 genuinely consistent days: if 7-day weight average and waist are both flat, reduce by 100–150 kcal/day. If weight is falling fast and strength/recovery are clearly worsening, add 100–150 kcal/day.

Muscle-gain system

Every exercise has a next target.

Log the load and reps. The next exposure tells you whether to add reps or add weight.

Progression algorithm

Step 1: choose a load that lands inside the rep range with the prescribed RIR.

Step 2: next session, beat the previous total by at least one clean rep somewhere.

Step 3: when every set reaches the top of the rep range, increase the smallest available load and rebuild reps.

Compounds: 1–2 RIR. Isolation: 0–2 RIR; only the final safe isolation set may reach technical failure.

Rule: form and back position outrank the number on the stack.

Simple food system

High protein without living in the kitchen.

The target is a repeatable deficit that still lets you train hard.

2,300calorie start
190–210 gprotein range
3–4 Lwater
8+ hrsleep target
Meal 1 · easiest breakfast

Smoothie: 0% Greek yogurt + chocolate protein powder + frozen fruit + banana + water/ice.

If you need more training fuel, add measured oats. If you are not hungry, do not force a giant breakfast.

Meal 2 · simple lunch

Choose a lean-protein meal built around grilled chicken/turkey/beef plus a controlled carb serving. Restaurant rule: prioritize protein first, then decide whether the carb/fat add-on is worth the calories.

Meal 3 · meal-prep dinner

8–10 oz cooked lean beef or chicken

Carb: measured rice OR potatoes

Vegetables: large serving of Brussels sprouts/other vegetables

If still hungry 30–60 min later: Greek yogurt + fruit, or Greek yogurt + a partial scoop of protein.

Rules that actually matter

No alcohol during the lock-in unless planned into calories. No cheat day. Restaurant meals are allowed when calories/protein still make sense. Do not starve the next day to compensate for one meal.
