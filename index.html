Progression

Save every working set. Next time, use the same weight and beat your total reps. Once every set reaches the top of its range, increase weight.

Compounds stay mostly 6 to 12 reps. Most hypertrophy work uses 8 to 15. Stable isolation work also uses 15 to 25. Cutting does not mean switching everything to light weights.
