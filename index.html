Biceps Specialization

The lifting plan is unchanged.

How to progress every curl

Double progression: keep the same weight until every set reaches the top of its rep range with strict form. Then add the smallest available weight.

Effort: most sets finish with 1–2 reps in reserve. Only the last set of an exercise may reach 0–1 reps in reserve.

Rest: 2–3 minutes on preacher, incline and Bayesian curls; 90–120 seconds on hammer and high-cable curls.
