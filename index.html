How to keep improving

The app tells you exactly what to beat under each exercise. Follow that target instead of guessing.

  1. Start at the bottom of the rep range. Pick a weight that lets you finish every set with the effort shown.
  2. Keep the same weight and add clean reps. Your next target is at least one more total rep than last time.
  3. When every set reaches the top of the range, add the smallest available weight jump next time and build the reps back up.
  4. Never count ugly reps. If form, range of motion, or your back position changes, the set is finished.
  5. If you regress once, repeat the weight. If you regress for two sessions in a row, reduce the load 5–10% and rebuild.
Example: 3 × 8–12 at 50 lb. Go from 10, 9, 8 to 10, 10, 8 or better. Once you reach 12, 12, 12, increase the weight next session.
