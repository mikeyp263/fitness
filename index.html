Progression + effort rules

Compounds: finish with 1–2 reps in reserve. Isolation: 0–2 RIR; only the final safe isolation set may reach technical failure.

Progression: when every working set reaches the top of the listed rep range cleanly, add the smallest available load.

Rest: 2–3 min compounds, 60–90 sec isolation.

Cut priority: keeping strength stable matters more than adding more exercises.
