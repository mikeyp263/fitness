Failure rule: compounds stop at 1–2 RIR. Most isolation work stays around 1 RIR, with the final safe isolation set allowed to reach 0–1 RIR. Abs finish at 0–1 RIR with controlled form. Technical failure means the next rep would require cheating, shortening range, or losing position.
Pain is not RIR. Sharp pain, back/hip pain that changes your movement, or a sudden joint pain means stop that exercise and use the listed alternative.
Exact morning order
  1. Hydrate: 16–24 oz water. Black coffee is optional if you already tolerate it.
  2. 5-minute warm-up: easy treadmill, bike or elliptical - just enough to feel warm.
  3. Ramp the first compound: 2–3 progressively heavier warm-up sets, none near failure.
  4. Do the full lifting session. Do not add random sets.
  5. If cardio is assigned: start Zone 2 immediately after lifting.
  6. Eat the big breakfast after the entire morning block.
Zone 2 instructions

Default: incline treadmill. Start around 3.0–3.4 mph and 6–10% incline, then adjust the incline until breathing is clearly elevated but you can still speak in short sentences. RPE about 3–4/10.

Bike or elliptical are equally acceptable. Do not turn Zone 2 into intervals. Do not grip the treadmill rails. If the incline irritates your back/hip, lower the incline or switch to bike/elliptical.

Progression

If every work set reaches the top of the rep range at the assigned RIR with clean form, add the smallest useful amount of weight next time. If reps fall below the range, keep the load until you own it. Log your top working loads in Notes.

Back / hip foundation

After Thursday cardio, do: pelvic tilts 1×15, cat-camel 1×8, half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch 2×40 sec/side, glute bridge 2×12, bird dog 2×6/side. Smooth reps only.
