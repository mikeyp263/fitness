The high-priority session gets your freshest hours: every normal lifting day starts with weights in the morning. Zone 2 is separated until later so it adds expenditure without taking quality away from the lift.

Four days are true two-a-days: morning weights, later easy cardio. Wednesday is morning legs only. Thursday is cardio/recovery. Sunday is complete formal rest.

Morning lifting rule

Wake, hydrate, and train. Black coffee is fine if you use caffeine. The default is to lift before breakfast, then eat the large breakfast immediately afterward. If fasted lifting clearly hurts your performance, the plan allows a small pre-lift protein/carb feed - preserving training quality matters more than keeping the fast perfect.

Effort + progression rules

Compounds: 1–2 reps in reserve. Isolation: 0–2 reps in reserve. Rest 2–3 minutes on compounds and 60–90 seconds on isolation. When you hit the top of a rep range on every set with clean form, add the smallest useful load next time.

Do not add random sets because you feel guilty. The goal is high-quality volume you can recover from while cutting.

Why abs are only 3 days

You get about 15 direct loaded ab sets/week. That is enough to actually progress the muscle while still recovering. More daily ab circuits would mostly add fatigue. Visible abs still require total fat loss; abdominal training does not selectively burn stomach fat.

Two-meal structure

AM lift → big breakfast → fast through work → dinner.

No lunch. No work snack. No refrigerated food at work. Water, black coffee and zero-calorie drinks/electrolytes are fine between meals.

Fasting rule: the main fast is from dinner through the next morning's post-lift breakfast, plus the no-calorie work block between breakfast and dinner. Fasting is a structure for adherence - the calorie deficit is what drives fat loss.
Performance override: if fasted morning lifting causes a clear performance drop, take 25 g whey + 1 banana 30–60 minutes before lifting. Count it toward the same 2,200 calories and subtract those calories from the post-lift breakfast/dinner. You still have no lunch.
Later cardio timing

On two-a-day days, do the prescribed Zone 2 later in the day, ideally separated from the morning lift by several hours. Before dinner is convenient if it fits your schedule; after dinner is also fine. Keep it conversational, not another hard workout.

Chicken dinner version

Use 8 oz cooked chicken breast instead of beef. Because chicken is leaner, you can use a larger rice/potato portion while keeping the same daily calories. Keep the Greek yogurt at dinner so the two-meal day still reaches the protein target.
