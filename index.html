Cut + Recomp · Aug 17–Sep 4

R14 Transformation

19-day push: visibly leaner waist while protecting strength and muscle.
Training priority

Keep muscle. Keep strength. Lose fat.

Four lifting days, two controlled recovery days, one complete rest day. No junk volume.

How every workout works

Compounds: stop with 1–2 reps in reserve. Isolation: 0–2 RIR; only the final safe isolation set may reach technical failure.

Rest: 2–3 minutes on compounds, 60–90 seconds on isolation work.

Progression: keep the same load until all working sets reach the top of the rep range cleanly, then add the smallest available load.

Cut rule: strength should stay broadly stable. If multiple lifts fall hard for two sessions, recovery or calories are too low.

Back rule: use supported/upright variations whenever possible. No loaded bending just to chase numbers.

Nutrition · 19 days

Protein stays high. Deficit stays controlled.

The goal is not the fastest scale loss. The goal is the most visible fat loss you can get without flattening out or sacrificing muscle.

Example lift day · ~2,350 kcal

Breakfast: 2 whole eggs + 250 g liquid egg whites + 60 g oatmeal + banana.

Lunch: 6 oz cooked chicken breast + 250 g cooked jasmine rice + vegetables + measured light sauce.

Meal 3: 300 g 0% Greek yogurt + 1 scoop whey + berries + 2 rice cakes.

Dinner: 6 oz lean beef, turkey, chicken or fish + 300–350 g potato or measured rice + vegetables.

Adjust carb portions to land on the calorie target. Do not cut protein to make calories fit.

Easy protein swaps

Chicken breast · 93–96% lean ground beef/turkey · shrimp · tuna · white fish · salmon · eggs · liquid egg whites · 0% Greek yogurt · cottage cheese · whey.

Use rice, potatoes, oats, fruit, bread/wraps and rice cakes as the main carb sources. Measure oils, nut butter, dressings and sauces.

Emergency day · no time

Immediately: protein shake + banana.

Take with you: high-protein Greek yogurt + protein bar.

Later: lean protein + measured rice/potato + vegetables.

Missing a cooked meal never becomes a reason to abandon the calorie or protein target.

19-day rules

  • 195 g protein every day.
  • Track everything, including oils, sauces, drinks and snacks.
  • No untracked cheat day.
  • No alcohol during the block.
  • Do not eat back watch-estimated exercise calories.
  • Keep restaurant meals rare and trackable.
  • Use zero/low-calorie drinks most of the time.
  • Keep sodium and water consistent instead of trying to dehydrate.
  • Sleep target: 8+ hours.

Adjustment checkpoints

Aug 24: compare the first 7-day average with your starting weight and waist.

If average weight is down roughly 0.5–1.0% of bodyweight/week and strength is stable, change nothing.

If loss is slower and waist is not moving, reduce about 150 kcal/day from carbs/fats. Keep protein at 195 g.

If loss is faster than ~1%/week and gym performance/recovery are clearly deteriorating, add 100–150 kcal/day, mainly carbs.

Aug 31: repeat the same check. No panic changes from one weigh-in.

Sep 2–4 · look your best without dehydration

Keep water and sodium normal. Avoid a huge restaurant meal, alcohol, excessive carbonation and foods you know bloat you.

Sep 3: upper-body session is reduced volume. Stay fresh.

Sep 4: take progress photos/waist measurement before a large meal. Optional short upper-body pump only.
