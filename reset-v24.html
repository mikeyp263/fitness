Programming rule:

Fast Pace comes first for the next 13 days. After the race, the endurance plan shifts toward a VO₂-focused block: lots of easy running, one true VO₂ interval day, one threshold day, long easy aerobic work, strides/sprints, and progressive mileage. Harder is not automatically better.

Block 1 · Fast Pace

Restore rhythm, race-specific intervals, threshold, taper.

Block 2 · Reload

1–2 weeks to restore consistent mileage after the race.

Block 3 · VO₂ Development

6–10 weeks. Mostly easy volume + one VO₂max interval session (generally 2–4 min reps) + one threshold stimulus + long run.

Retest

Use repeatable race results and, when practical, a real lab VO₂ test. Treat watch VO₂ as a trend, not absolute truth.
