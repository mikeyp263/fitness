WORKOUT EVOLUTION

Easy first. Specific later.

Weeks 1–5 · Foundation

Easy running only, plus short relaxed strides. You are rebuilding frequency and tissue tolerance.

Weeks 6–8 · Economy

Short relaxed hill/repetition work with full recovery. Week 8 includes a 3K calibration if healthy.

Weeks 9–12 · Threshold

Cruise intervals progress from 3×5 min to 2×8 min to a controlled 20-minute tempo.

Weeks 14–18 · 5K Development

Friday alternates Daniels-style Interval and Threshold work. Tuesday stays mostly easy/strides so Wednesday Legs remains productive.

Week 19 · Taper / Race Window

Use it for a February 5K if the build is green. If not, continue training and choose a later race.

PLANNED RUNNING PEAK · 34 MI/WEEK

Enough volume to rebuild serious aerobic fitness while remaining compatible with five lifting days. We do not need to rush back to your former 70-mile weeks.

EASY

HR 135–150 · RPE 2–3 · conversational. Pace is secondary.

QUALITY

Only one true hard run per week for most of this build. Threshold and Interval work are added after the foundation exists.

LOAD GATE

If running starts reducing Wednesday leg performance, causing persistent pain, or making lifting numbers regress for multiple sessions, we hold or cut running mileage before sacrificing the lifting priority.
